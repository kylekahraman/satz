**Development features:** the current proposal implementations require the [source checkout](/satz/guides/development/). The published `0.1.1` DFG template is an older work in progress.

## Choose the right starting point

| Proposal | Development support |
| --- | --- |
| DFG research grant | Form 53.01, revision 09/26; German and English starters |
| NWO Talent Programme | Source-based 2026 Veni, Vidi, and Vici profiles; coverage varies by stage and domain |
| General research or project proposal | Flexible title, summary, sections, budget, and planning components |

For DFG, the implementation includes separate page-budget checks for the description and supplementary sections, publication helpers, and current/total page headers. Layout comparisons use the referenced LaTeX template; they do not establish compliance for every funding call.

For NWO, this is **not every NWO form**. Represented profiles cover Veni/Vidi pre-proposals across four domains, Vici pre-proposals in Science/SSH/AES, and full Vici across four domains. Full Veni/Vidi and the unavailable ZonMw Vici pre-proposal are not represented. Check the exact scheme, stage, domain, and year before choosing a starter.

## Find a starter and the detailed instructions

- [DFG and general proposal guide](https://github.com/kylekahraman/satz/blob/main/docs/proposals.md)
- [NWO profile coverage and sources](https://github.com/kylekahraman/satz/blob/main/docs/nwo-proposals.md)
- [Work packages, Gantt charts, and budget components](https://github.com/kylekahraman/satz/blob/main/docs/proposal-planning.md)

Use the official call's current instructions when submitting. The helpers support authoring and layout checks; they cannot check the scientific content or every call-specific requirement.
